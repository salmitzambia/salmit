YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy and analytics

Quotation requests

The quotation form prepares a message in WhatsApp. It does not send the request until you review and send that message. You can also contact Salmit Zambia directly by email or phone.

Website chat

The chat widget prepares a message for WhatsApp. The message is sent to Salmit only if you review it and press send in WhatsApp. Chat text is masked from Microsoft Clarity recordings.

Website analytics

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are inactive until their project IDs are configured. Once enabled, they load only after you accept analytics in the preference prompt. If you decline, neither tool is loaded. Your choice is saved in this browser so the prompt does not appear on every page.

Google Analytics measures page visits and site usage. Microsoft Clarity helps us understand how visitors use the site through interaction insights, heatmaps and session recordings. We also record general actions such as service interest and clicks on quotation and contact links. The quotation form is explicitly masked in Clarity, and form inputs are masked by default. For information about Google’s processing, see Google’s privacy information for partner sites. Learn more about Microsoft privacy practices.

After analytics is enabled, you can change your choice using "Analytics settings" in the footer.
