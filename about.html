About Us

SALMIT Zambia is a dynamic and innovative Zambian-owned Information Technology company specializing in the supply of IT equipment, IT services, consultancy, systems installation, Artificial Intelligence (AI) consultancy, database solutions, and professional IT training services.

Established with a vision of delivering modern, reliable, and cost-effective technology solutions, SALMIT Zambia is committed to meeting the evolving technological needs of businesses, institutions, NGOs, and individuals across Zambia.

At SALMIT Zambia, we believe technology is a key driver for growth, efficiency, security, and innovation.

Our experienced team is dedicated to delivering professional services and high quality products that help organizations improve productivity, strengthen security, and achieve operational excellence.
